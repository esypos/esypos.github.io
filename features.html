Free Offline POS Software for Small Shops

EsyPOS is a free point of sale (POS) app for grocery stores, pharmacies, fashion shops, restaurants, mobile shops and wholesalers. Bill faster, track stock, manage customer dues and see your profit. It works offline and backs up to your own Google Drive.

Who is EsyPOS for?

Grocery and convenience stores · Pharmacies · Clothing and fashion shops · Restaurants and cafés · Mobile and electronics shops · Wholesalers and distributors · Any small business that needs simple billing and stock control.

How it works

1. Open EsyPOS and sign in with Google. 2. Add your products and start billing. 3. Install it on your home screen and keep selling, even offline.

Frequently asked questions

Is EsyPOS really free?

Yes. EsyPOS is free to use, with no monthly fee, no per-device fee and no hidden charges.

Is EsyPOS open source?

Yes. EsyPOS is an open-source project. Anyone can run their own business with their own Google account, with no third-party company involved.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the first load the app opens and sells offline. Your backup syncs to Google Drive when you are online again.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, plus an automatic backup in a private folder of your own Google Drive. EsyPOS has no server and cannot see your data.

Do I have to sign in with Google every time I open the app?

No. EsyPOS opens straight away on your device. It renews its Google Drive connection in the background, and if Google needs a confirmation you just tap Reconnect. Selling is never blocked.

Which devices are supported?

Any modern browser on Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac and Linux. You can install it from the browser like a native app.

Can I scan barcodes and print receipts?

Yes. Scan with the phone camera in Chrome on Android, or use a USB or Bluetooth barcode scanner on any device. Print on 58 mm or 80 mm paper or on a Bluetooth thermal printer.

Can I track customer dues and suppliers?

Yes. Each customer and supplier has a ledger with dues, payments and reminders by WhatsApp or SMS.

What if I change or lose my phone?

Sign in with the same Google account and your shop data returns from your Drive backup.

Does it support my local currency and language?

Product and customer names can be written in any language, and amounts use the ৳ currency format with lakh-crore grouping.

Is my business data safe?

Data stays on your device and in a private app folder of your Google Drive that EsyPOS alone can read. You can also lock the app with a PIN.
