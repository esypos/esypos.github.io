Sales Cart

Free offline POS software for small shops and growing businesses

EsyPOS is a free, open-source point of sale (POS) system that runs in your browser and installs like an app on Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac and Linux. Make bills in seconds, scan barcodes with your phone camera, track inventory and expiry dates, manage customer dues and supplier payments, record expenses and see profit reports. It keeps working when the internet is down, and your data is backed up to your own Google Drive: no server, no monthly fee and no third-party company in the middle.

Fast billing and checkout

Search products or scan barcodes, apply discounts and tax, hold bills and accept cash, card, mobile banking or due.

Inventory and stock alerts

Track stock levels, low-stock and expiry alerts, product photos, and import or export products with CSV.

Customer dues and suppliers

Keep a ledger for every customer and supplier, collect payments and send reminders by WhatsApp or SMS.

Expenses and profit reports

See net sales, gross and net profit, collections and a 7-day sales chart on one dashboard.

Works offline, installs anywhere

An installable progressive web app (PWA) that opens and sells without internet and syncs when you are back online.

Private by design

Your shop data stays on your device and in one backup file in your own Google Drive. No ads, no tracking.

Who uses EsyPOS?

Grocery and convenience stores, pharmacies, fashion and gift shops, mobile phone shops, restaurants and cafes, wholesalers and any small business that needs a simple, free billing and inventory app.
