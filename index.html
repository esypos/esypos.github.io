EsyPOS – Free Offline POS Software for Small Shops

EsyPOS is a free point of sale (POS) app for grocery stores, pharmacies, fashion shops, restaurants, mobile shops and wholesalers. Make bills fast, scan barcodes, track stock and expiry, manage customer dues and suppliers, record expenses and see profit reports. It works offline, installs like a native app on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac, and backs up your data to your own Google Drive. No server, no monthly fee. EsyPOS is open source: run your own business with your own Google account, with no third-party company involved.
